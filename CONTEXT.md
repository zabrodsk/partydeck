# Social card games

Friends play card games using their phones for their own cards and actions. A shared table display can show the game to the room.

## Language

**Table display**:
An optional shared screen showing public game information to everyone in the room. It is separate from any player's private hand.

**Host**:
The player who manages a room, including starting and pausing play. Hosting is a role, not a particular display device.

**Room**:
A group of players who join using the same invitation code or QR code to play together.

**Player screen**:
A player's phone view of their own hand, chip stack and controls. In shared-screen mode it does not repeat public table information.

**Shared-screen mode**:
Play with public game information on a separate table display and private hands on phones.

**Phone-only mode**:
The proposed fallback in which each phone also displays public game information because there is no shared display.

**Virtual chips**:
The fictional units used for betting during a game. They are not real money.
_Avoid_: Cash

**Profile**:
A player's saved identity for statistics and achievements across games.

**Guest**:
A player who joins with a nickname without signing in.

**Seat**:
A player's place at a table, associated with their stack and participation in the current hand.

**Stack**:
The virtual chips a player has available at the table.

**Rebuy**:
A host-approved replenishment of a player's virtual chips between hands.

**Achievement**:
A cosmetic milestone earned from completed play. It does not change cards, odds or chip awards.

**Persistent virtual bankroll**:
A future balance of virtual chips that a player carries between games.

**External stakes tracking**:
A possible future record of real stakes that friends settle outside the app.
